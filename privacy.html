Privacy Policy for SFina

Last Updated: November 2026

SFina respects your privacy and is designed with a local-first architecture. This policy explains how your information is handled.

1. Local Storage

All your financial records, budgets, and settings are stored locally on your device. SFina does not operate external servers to collect or store your personal financial data.

2. Google Drive Integration (drive.appdata)

If you choose to sign in with your Google account for backup purposes, SFina requests access exclusively to its own hidden application folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata scope). This access is used solely to save and restore your backup file (sfina-backup.json). SFina cannot view, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

3. No Data Sharing & Contact

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties or external analytics services.
